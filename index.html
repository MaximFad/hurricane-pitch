What we are building now

The next game

Idle Fighter

Idle RPG · iOS and Android · Free to play with ads · MVP in development

A fighter trains, gears up and chains moves from different martial arts into combos to become champion.

Combo moves

A six-card move deck with its own gacha. Same-rarity moves chain into combos; duplicates merge.

Martial arts

Players pick a discipline: boxing or karate at launch. Mixed-style leagues reward the best combo.

Unique fighters

Fighters drop from chests with random stats and abilities, so there is always a better roll to chase.

Target metrics

Day 1 retention of 35%+ and 35-40 minutes of playtime, benchmarked on the closest competitor.
